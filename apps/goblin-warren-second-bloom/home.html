This is not a video. Below is the actual game engine — deterministic, seeded, replaying Level 1 forever. The player kindles, drags a twig, fails to lift the heavy wood… then marks it. The mark is not a command. It is a glow in the world. Bram notices it himself.

  • Your body. Hold the ember — warmth answers.
  • Your hands. Drag the twig — direct help.
  • Your limit. The big wood will not move.
  • Your mark. A signal in the world — not an order.
  • His choice. Bram pauses, turns his head, walks, carries.

for the curious — what is this, really?

A Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion system — two chemicals feeding and consuming each other under pure mathematics (Turing, 1952), computed live on your GPU. It is also how ants, cells, and modern AI agent swarms coordinate: not by command, but by signals left in a shared world. The game's word for it is a mark. Science calls it stigmergy. You just did it.
