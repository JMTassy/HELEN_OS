## 2. MAYOR RULINGS

1. **Level 1 is now "The Dying Fire."** Zone: campfire clearing. Active: Bram.
   Background: Lulu. Verbs: `KINDLE` (hold — direct), `DRAG` (direct),
   `MARK` (delegated). The five-phase 90-second arc replaces Cracked Root.
   Cracked Root + Dry Seedlings move to the garden zone as Level 2+ needs —
   nothing is lost, only resequenced.
2. **Invariant chain updated, not weakened:**
   `MARK → trace_peak → bram_paused → bram_oriented → bram_moving →
   bram_arrived → bram_carries_wood → fire_fed → need_resolved`
   plus new hard invariants: `DRAG on heavy wood fails deterministically`,
   `glow hint appears only after a failed drag (taught by causality, not timer)`,
   `KINDLE/DRAG never assign Bram a task`.
3. **Input abstraction is now mandatory architecture** (`src/core/events`):
   every physical input is an abstract event (`KINDLE`, `DRAG`, `MARK`).
   V0 bindings: hold / pointer-drag / tap-hold. The composted tactile pack
   (blow/shake/tilt) becomes alternate bindings later — zero rearchitecting.
4. **Cast lock for V0:** Bram (active), Lulu (L2), Pip (recorder, L3).
   Moss & Grim banked. No sixth goblin before the campaign ships.
5. **Trace schema locked for V0:** `{type: WARNING|RESOURCE|RELATIONAL,
   intensity, decay_rate, affinity_tags, source, position, observers}` —
   Relational dormant until L4.
6. **Success test unchanged** (mission §4/§16): a new player states that MARK
   made Bram notice and act, while direct verbs fixed things themselves.
   The Dying Fire makes this *easier* to pass, because the world explains
   why delegation exists.

## 6. ADDENDUM — key-factors analysis + two visual boards (operator supplement)

### 6.1 Key factors of success (Goblin_Grok/Helen_GPT ranking) — HAL response

The ranking itself is sound and grafts cleanly: hook first, causal clarity
second, gentle ramp as the retention threat. Two rulings sharpen against it:

- **The mic stays composted for V0 — by the analysis's own numbers.** Its
  most critical factor (#1 hook) is bet on blow detection, whose confidence
  the same table rates **Medium** with the note "if blowing feels weak,
  delayed, or inconsistent, the entire hook collapses." A first playable is
  never built on the lowest-confidence technology in the stack. Hold-to-KINDLE
  preserves the hook's *shape* (dark → sustained gesture → warmth blooms);
  breath becomes a binding upgrade after the causal core is proven.
- **Comprehension test wording — grafted.** Level 1 passes when a player can
  say, unprompted: "I woke the fire myself. I couldn't reach the big wood.
  When I marked it, Bram brought it." (V0: "with my touch"; tactile pack
  later restores "with my breath".)
- Replay lever grafted as L1 stretch goal: multiple valid orderings
  (kindle-heavy vs early-MARK) must both complete under the same seed rules.
