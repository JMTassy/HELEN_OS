## 0. Essence (the one line)
**AI made production free. UZIK sells what it never touched: craft, coherence, and proof.**
Premium French creative studio, est. 2000. Governed AI, receipts over claims, craft over slop.
Polytechnique precision × Corsican sunlight. Restraint is the brand.

## 1. Voice & tone (write like JMT)
- **Register:** editorial, confident, concrete over abstract. Short, punchy sentences. Receipts over adjectives.
- **Yes:** "I don't optimize for output. I optimize for truth." · "Ten mirrors weigh less than one open window."
- **No:** hype, "revolutionary/innovative", buzzword stacking, invented metrics, hedge-less superlatives.
- **Claims discipline:** never assert a number or outcome without a source. `claim ⊬ proof`. If unverified, say so.
- **Warmth:** human, never corporate-cold; sharp, never mean.

## 2. Palette (strict, restrained — luxury not tech)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink-black` | `#0a0a0b` | primary background (near-black, warm) |
| `--paper` | `#F2EBDD` | primary text / warm off-white |
| `--gold` | `#C8A24C` | ONE accent — a rule, an emphasized word, a signature. Sparingly. |
| `--gold-dim` | `#A8823F` | kickers, hairlines |
| `--indigo` | `#1b1930` | deep night wash / secondary panels |
| `--violet` | `#8a7fb8` | rare atmospheric accent (Garden night scenes) |
| `--graphite` | `#141821` | cards / panels |
Rule: **one gold accent per view.** Never neon. Never gradients-as-decoration. Governance color (if any) stays semantic.

## 3. Typography
- **Display / headlines:** refined high-contrast **serif** (Didot / Garamond / EB Garamond feel), large, generous leading, letter-spacing ~0.02–0.12em for caps. This carries JMT's voice.
- **Labels / kickers / captions:** fine tracked-out **mono or grotesque sans**, uppercase, letter-spacing ~0.18–0.28em.
- **Body:** clean serif or humanist sans, high legibility.
- Hierarchy through **size + space**, not color noise.

## 4. Layout
- **Vast negative space.** Fewer elements, bigger. Editorial, left-aligned or centered.
- Source-Atlas influence where governance is shown: center = object, margins = commentary, top = state.
- Grid-anchored, calm. Restraint reads as premium (Hermès/LVMH register), never busy.

## 5. Motion (HyperFrames / video — motion doctrine)
- **One continuous camera**, not a stack of slides. How a scene exits = how the next enters (same axis/direction, cut mid-motion). Default current = **LEFT**.
- **No idle wobble** — no breathe/float/glow-pulse to fill time. Every beat *performs* (staged reveal, camera-with-intent, sequenced UI).
- **Stillness before climax** (a ~0.4s dramatic comma). Reserve **upward** motion for the conclusion.
- Eases: `power2.out` / `back.out(1.35)` for entrances; **forbidden:** `bounce.out`, `elastic.out`, idle sine loops.
- Cut to the voice (word-timings), not to a metronome.

## 6. Imagery
- Premium, editorial, **anti-AI-slop.** Cinematic stills, real texture, restraint. Warm night palettes for Garden/Warren scenes (indigo/violet + ember gold).
- Never generic-slick, never stocky, never neon-cyber cliché.

## 7. The governance stamp (non-negotiable)
Every UZIK/HELEN artifact carries: `authority: false · non_sovereign · dream/expression, not admission.`
An artifact is a **candidate**, never a decision. `render ⊬ admitted`. Facts are cited from sources/RAG,
never asserted from a model's weights. This is the differentiator: *governed AI — claims with receipts.*

## 8. Anti-patterns (reject on sight)
- Neon/tech-startup gradients · buzzword hype · invented metrics · more-than-one-accent-color · idle motion to fill time · generic AI aesthetic · a claim without a source · treating a rendered artifact as authority.
