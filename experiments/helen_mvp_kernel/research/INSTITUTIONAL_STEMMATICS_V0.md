## What this module adds — and what it does NOT

The J1 UZIK census already found the corpus is heterogeneous witness
classes, not one homogeneous corpus. Stemmatics weighs them by
genealogy. But most of the machinery is **already sealed elsewhere**,
and honesty requires naming that rather than re-minting it:

| stemmatics piece | already in the kernel as |
|---|---|
| root-family collapse (5 docs → 1 root) | `root_normalizer.py` (archaeologist skill) |

## The new pieces

1. **Epistemic density** — `rho_epi = |R_independent| / |D_representations|`.
   Five UZIK artifacts of the same "Business Agility" claim from one
   root → `rho_epi = 0.2`, `amplification_illusion = True`. A huge
   archive with `rho_epi << 1` holds little independent knowledge.
   **UZIK-NONAMPLIFICATION-01:** `5 × "UZIK says X" ⊬ 5 × evidence`.

2. **Adversarial retrieval** — rank by `ExpectedFrontierChange`, not
   `Similarity(query, d)`:

        d* = argmax_d ExpectedFrontierChange(d)

   A contradictory economics sheet (similarity 0.40, frontier-change
   0.80) outranks a fourth marketing assertion (similarity 0.95,
   frontier-change 0.01). A retrieval configured `max_similarity` is
   refused (`E_SIMILARITY_BLIND_RETRIEVAL`) — it is blind to the
   discriminating document. HELEN sometimes seeks the *least
   convenient* UZIK document.

3. **Negative memory is first-class** — five witness classes
   (SUCCESS, FAILURE, PARTIAL, UNKNOWN, UNRESOLVED).
   `Capability(C) = f(Successes, Failures, BoundaryConditions,
   Counterexamples)`. A capability claimed from successes with no
   witnessed failure or boundary is `E_SURVIVORSHIP_CAPABILITY`. The
   institutional question shifts from "what did UZIK do well" to
   "under exactly what conditions did mechanism C work, fail, or
   become unidentified."

4. **The anti-mythology chain** — six non-implications the graph may
   never collapse:

        archive mass    ⊬ knowledge
        repetition      ⊬ corroboration
        corporate claim ⊬ capability
        past capability ⊬ present capability
        learned pattern ⊬ authority
        authority       ⊬ effect

   And **constitutional forgetting**: `warranted_2016 ⊬ available_2026
   ⊬ authorized_2026`. The frontier is time-indexed — keep the
   historical record, do not inherit its present authority. This is
   forgetting without erasure.

5. **The Institutional Witness Graph type** —
   `IWG = (D, C, R, E, τ, Ω, F*)`. A claim's support is counted in
   independent **roots R net of contradictions**, never in
   representations D. `iwg_claim_support` returns SUPPORTED /
   CONTESTED / UNSUPPORTED with `promoted = False` by default — the
   extraction algorithm emits this typed object instead of a
   similarity-ranked chunk list.
