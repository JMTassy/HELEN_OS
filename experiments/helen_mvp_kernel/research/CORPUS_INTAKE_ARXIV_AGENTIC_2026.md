## The genuine chiddush (a real gap, now encoded)

HELEN had verification-as-edge-gate (producer ≠ approver) but **no
notion of verification LATENCY or corrector PLACEMENT**. The corpus
supplies it:

> "Delayed Verification Destabilizes Multi-Agent LLM Belief:
> Instability Thresholds and Optimal Corrector Placement" (2606.27409)
> — false claims propagate during the verifier's delay; there is an
> instability threshold and an optimal placement.

Verification is not only WHO but WHEN. Encoded as
`execution_graph.verification_placement(unverified_propagation_steps,
max_latency, is_terminal)` (+1 test, gate probe extended): a claim
propagating through more unverified hops than the latency bound is
`E_DELAYED_VERIFICATION` — propagated unverified state masquerading as
reviewed. **Grade discipline:** the corpus finding is REPORTED, so the
encoding is the STRUCTURAL principle (latency matters and must be
bounded), NOT a measured threshold — no fabricated number entered the
kernel. This composes with the existing verification edge: producer ≠
approver AND corrector within latency.
