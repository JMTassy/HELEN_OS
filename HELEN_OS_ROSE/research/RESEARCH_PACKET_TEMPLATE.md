# Research packet template

Use for any bounded investigation (a `RESEARCH` outcome, a Phase-F funding
verification, a discovery synthesis). Keep to one page.

```text
RESEARCH_ID:        RQ-###
QUESTION:           the single question this packet answers
WHY_NOW:            which decision or assumption depends on it
METHOD:             how the answer will be obtained (interviews, documents,
                    measurement, external sources)
EVIDENCE_TARGET:    the class the answer should reach (usually E3/E4/E5)
TIME_BOX:           hard limit in days or hours
OUTPUT:             memo path + evidence_register entries (EV-###)
STATUS:             PROPOSED | RESEARCHED | HOLD | REJECTED
```

Rules:

- One question per packet. A second question is a second packet.
- The output always includes evidence register entries with `source`,
  `date`, `scope`, `limitations` — an answer without limitations noted is
  not done.
- External claims (funding eligibility, market sizes, institutional
  programs) stay E0 until checked against an independent source (E4) or a
  formal document (E5).
