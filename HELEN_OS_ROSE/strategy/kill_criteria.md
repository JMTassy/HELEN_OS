# Kill criteria — initial wedge (Option A, hospitality decision twin)

**STATUS: PROPOSED — REQUIRES ROSE DECISION.** Kill criteria are decided
before work starts so the day-90 review is a measurement, not a mood.

## Hard stops (recommend `REJECT` at review)

- K1 — Problem: fewer than 50% of ≥10 completed discovery interviews
  confirm a real, budgeted decision pain (falsifies A1).
- K2 — Access: fewer than 8 interviews completed after 30 genuine outreach
  contacts and 6 weeks (falsifies A2).
- K3 — Willingness to pay: zero paid pilots or signed LOIs from at least 5
  concrete proposals by day 90 (falsifies A5).
- K4 — Value: the internal Casa Cielo pilot shows no measurable decision
  improvement after 4 operating weeks (falsifies A3).

## Narrow (recommend `REVISE`)

- N1 — Pain confirmed but concentrated in one sub-segment (e.g., retreats
  rather than hotels): narrow the wedge to that segment.
- N2 — Operators value the human advisory more than the twin/dashboard:
  reshape as decision-advisory with lighter tooling.
- N3 — Sustainability decisions dominate guest-experience decisions (or
  vice versa): cut the weaker half from the offer.

## Revise the system, not just the wedge

- S1 — If two consecutive weekly reviews show work happening without
  packets or decisions, the operating system itself is failing; run a
  dedicated sovereign review on process.

## Personal sustainability

- P1 — If the wedge consistently drains Rose's energy or conflicts with
  her values in ways the weekly review keeps surfacing, that is a valid
  strategic signal and grounds for `REVISE` or `REJECT`, independent of
  commercial metrics.

Only Rose retires or waives a kill criterion, via a ledger entry.
