# Current strategic thesis

It is the bootstrap strategy function's best recommendation from available

## CONTEXT

Rose builds at the intersection of AI, digital twins, wellness and human
transformation, high-end hospitality, ecological regeneration, community,
research, public thought leadership, and fundraising. Cielo Impact is the
primary strategic vehicle. Adjacent activities include Casa Cielo, Île
d'Aval, hospitality/retreat work, a public brand, corporate structures
(including a stated brand sequence: amuseinparis, then Cielo Impact, then
Lemuria Globals — E1, see EV-004), research programs, and personal matters
kept in their own privacy partitions.

The main strategic risk is fragmentation: many credible ideas, several
brands, and no single evidence-backed wedge.

## OBJECTIVE (twelve months)

One coherent objective, proposed:

> Within twelve months, establish Cielo Impact as a credible
> decision-intelligence studio for regenerative luxury hospitality — with
> two or three paid pilot engagements, one reproducible pilot methodology,
> a funding-application dossier built on real pilot evidence, and a public
> narrative that never outruns the receipts.

Everything else (new brands, platform, community products) is sequenced
behind that objective, not beside it.

## OPTIONS (three, no more)

**Option A — Hospitality Decision Twin wedge (recommended).**
A narrow service-first offer for high-end hospitality properties: a
"decision twin" of one property — a structured model of guest experience,
wellbeing signals, and environmental/energy signals — delivered first as an
expert engagement with a lightweight dashboard, not a platform. Casa Cielo
serves as internal testbed before any external engagement.
Pros: touches digital twins and decision intelligence credibly; testable
with real properties; creates proprietary learning; matches Rose's luxury,
ecological, human orientation; no platform build required.
Cons: services revenue scales slowly; requires access to properties.

**Option B — Wellness/biohacking community and content business first.**
Build the public brand, community, memberships, digital products.
Pros: fastest to publish, uses Rose's existing expertise.
Cons: crowded market; weak connection to Cielo Impact's digital-twin
thesis; revenue per unit low; risks premature branding before positioning
evidence exists.

**Option C — Coach/expert knowledge-system product (B2B SaaS) first.**
Build an AI knowledge system for coaches/experts as a product.
Pros: product leverage, recurring revenue potential.
Cons: requires the biggest unvalidated build; distant from hospitality
edge; highest burn before first evidence.

Comparison criteria: time-to-first-evidence, capital required before
evidence, fit to Rose's distinctive assets, path to high-value clients,
proprietary learning created. Option A wins on four of five; Option B wins
only on time-to-first-content, which is not the same as evidence.

## RECOMMENDATION

Adopt Option A as the **initial commercial wedge — explicitly a
hypothesis, not a validated business**. Options B elements (public
narrative, thought leadership) continue as support, feeding discovery
conversations rather than as a separate business. Option C is deferred to
the opportunity register.

## EVIDENCE_REQUIRED

- 10–15 discovery interviews with operators/GMs/owners in high-end
  hospitality and retreats; problem confirmed in ≥ 50% (would raise A1
  toward E4).
- One internal pilot on Casa Cielo producing a measurable decision
  improvement (E3).
- At least one external property signing a paid pilot or letter of intent
  (E5) within 90 days of outreach start.

## RISKS

- Access risk: the network may not yield enough qualified conversations.
- Positioning risk: "digital twin" language may read as tech hype to
  hospitality buyers; the offer must be framed in operator outcomes.
- Focus risk: the brand sequence (F4) may pull effort into naming and
  entity creation before evidence exists. Sovereign tension — flagged for
  Rose in the decision list below.
- Privacy risk: hospitality pilots may involve guest data; packets must
  declare `PARTNER_RESTRICTED` handling from day one.

## PUBLIC NARRATIVE (proposed, non-overclaiming)

> Cielo Impact builds decision intelligence for regenerative luxury —
> connecting AI and digital-twin methods with human and environmental
> signals so that exceptional places can make better decisions for guests,

intelligence + regenerative luxury. It must not claim technology in
production, existing clients, scientific breakthroughs, or institutional
backing while none is in the evidence register.
