# Ninety-day evidence plan

**STATUS: PROPOSED — REQUIRES ROSE DECISION.** Dates are relative to the
`GO` on the wedge decision (R-002 candidate), not to the bootstrap date.

The plan buys evidence, not deliverables. Each phase ends with an evidence
register update and a continuation check against
`strategy/kill_criteria.md`.

## Phase A — Weeks 1–2: Discovery setup

- Build the outreach list: 30 named prospects (owners, GMs, directors of
  wellbeing/sustainability at high-end properties and retreats).
- Write the interview guide: decision pain, current tools, budget reality,
  sustainability pressures, guest-experience measurement.
- Milestone: list + guide exist (E2); first 5 conversations scheduled.

## Phase B — Weeks 3–6: Problem validation

- Run 10–15 interviews. Notes filed per interview; claims tagged E1/E4.
- Mid-point synthesis at interview 8: is the pain real, budgeted, urgent?
- Milestone: synthesis memo with a confirm/deny verdict on assumption A1.

## Phase C — Weeks 4–8 (overlapping): Internal prototype on Casa Cielo

- Define one decision loop (e.g., energy vs guest-comfort scheduling, or
  wellbeing programming vs occupancy) and instrument it simply.
- Deliver a lightweight decision dashboard or structured weekly decision
  memo — service-first, no platform.
- Milestone: one measured decision improvement, reproducible write-up (E3).

## Phase D — Weeks 6–10: Pilot-partner outreach

- Convert the best discovery conversations into pilot proposals: paid
  engagement, bounded scope, `PARTNER_RESTRICTED` data handling.
- Target: 5 proposals out, aiming for 1 signed paid pilot or LOI (E5).
- Milestone: at least one signed document, or a documented explanation.

## Phase E — Weeks 8–12: Measurement

- Define the pilot metrics before starting: decision cycle time, guest
  satisfaction proxy, energy/resource delta, operator hours saved.
- Milestone: metric baseline captured for any signed pilot.

## Phase F — Weeks 8–12: Funding preparation

- Shortlist non-dilutive routes. Candidates to investigate — **all
  eligibility and availability claims below are unverified (E0) and marked
  for external verification**:
  - Bpifrance innovation support (French, non-dilutive)
  - France 2030 calls relevant to tourism/ecology/AI
  - ADEME programs (ecological transition)
  - Horizon Europe / EIC pathways (European)
  - Regional programs where the operating entity is domiciled
- Milestone: verification memo per route (raises each to E4), plus one
  dossier skeleton reusing pilot evidence.

## Phase G — Weeks 2–12: Public narrative

- Publish the one-page positioning; then a small steady cadence (e.g., one
  substantial essay every two weeks) drawn from discovery learnings.
- Rule: nothing published may exceed the evidence register.
- Milestone: positioning page live; 4–6 pieces published.

## Phase H — Week 12–13: Continue-or-kill review

- SOVEREIGN_REVIEW assembles the evidence; Rose records one of
  `GO` (scale the wedge) / `REVISE` (narrow it) / `REJECT` (kill it) /
  `RESEARCH` (extend discovery, once only).

## Funding and partnership map (categories, not commitments)

Kept separate by kind — nothing here is an existing relationship:

- Non-dilutive French funding: see Phase F list (unverified).
- European programs: Horizon Europe / EIC (unverified).
- Research: 1–2 academic groups in wellbeing measurement or building/
  environment modeling as prospective collaborators after the pilot.
- Luxury/hospitality pilots: the Phase D prospect list.
- Strategic investors: out of scope until pilot evidence exists.
- Commercial clients: the pilot properties themselves are the first
  candidates.
