# Domain: Corporate

Default privacy class: `CONFIDENTIAL_STRATEGY` (formal filings once public:
`PUBLIC`; anything with counsel: `LEGAL_PRIVATE`).

Company structures and entity planning: existing structures (e.g., AI
Productions or related companies), the stated brand/entity sequence
(amuseinparis → Cielo Impact → Lemuria Globals), domiciliation, and
cap-table questions.

Rules:

- Entity creation is expensive focus-wise; it follows a ledger `GO`, never
  precedes it (see priority stack STOP list).
- Legal and tax specifics belong with professionals; this domain stores
  decision context and pointers, not legal advice.
- No entity's existence or status is asserted here without a registry
  extract or formal document reference (E5).
