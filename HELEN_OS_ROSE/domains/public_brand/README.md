# Domain: Public brand

Default privacy class: `PUBLIC` (drafts before release:
`INTERNAL_BUSINESS`).

Rose's public narrative, thought leadership, essays, talks, and social
presence — including the ROSE persona work and the brand sequence intent
(amuseinparis, Cielo Impact, Lemuria Globals — timing governed by the
R-003 candidate decision).

Rules:

- Nothing published may exceed the evidence register. The claim linter's
  vocabulary (`partner`, `customer`, `funded`, `deployed`, `scientifically
  proven`, ...) is exactly the list of words that get founders in trouble.
- Public content never automatically inherits private context — no
  personal, medical, legal, or financial details migrate here without an
  explicit Rose decision.
- Wellness content avoids medical claims; phrasing stays within
  non-clinical wellbeing language (see open question Q8).
