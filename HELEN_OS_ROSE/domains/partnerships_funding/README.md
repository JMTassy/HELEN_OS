# Domain: Partnerships and funding

Default privacy class: `CONFIDENTIAL_STRATEGY` (signed documents:
`PARTNER_RESTRICTED` or `LEGAL_PRIVATE` as applicable).

Prospect lists, outreach state, funding-route verification memos, research
collaborations, and investor material.

Rules:

- A relationship claim requires paper: `partner` means a signed agreement
  (E5); before that, the word is "prospect" or "conversation".
- Funding-route eligibility is E0 until verified against the program's own
  documentation (E4) or a formal response (E5). Verification memos follow
  `research/RESEARCH_PACKET_TEMPLATE.md`.
- Financial terms of any agreement stay in `FINANCIAL_PRIVATE` per the
  partition rules; this domain keeps the relationship state, not the
  numbers.
