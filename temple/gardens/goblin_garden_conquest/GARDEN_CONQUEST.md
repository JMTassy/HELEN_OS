## The Law at the Threshold

The door may open.  
The world may simulate.  
The goblin may guide.  
But the ledger does not move.

DREAM_OF_CONQUEST is a Temple simulation of a knowledge-to-power world.  
It may model factions, territories, quests, resources, and strategy.  
It may not mutate HELEN's ledger, define HELEN's canon, or act as kernel machinery.

Temple may simulate.  
Temple may teach.  
Temple may not govern.

## What This Garden Contains

- A world model for DREAM_OF_CONQUEST (simulation layer only)
- Faction sigils, territory maps, quest seeds, resource models
- A goblin guide protocol for navigating the dream world
- WULmoji bulletins in CONQUEST dialect
- A 25-epoch Temple autoresearch simulation (non-sovereign / Temple-only)
- A validator that enforces all of the above
