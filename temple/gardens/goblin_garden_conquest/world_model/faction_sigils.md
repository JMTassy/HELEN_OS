```
THE FOUR FACTIONS OF DREAM_OF_CONQUEST

  🌹 THE ARCHIVE ROSE
     Domain: Historical knowledge, documentation, source-provenance
     Power mechanic: receipted claims from verified sources
     Weakness: slow to act, needs proof before moving
     Sigil: 🌹  (rose — beauty from careful cultivation)

  🌀 THE SPIRAL GUILD
     Domain: Pattern recognition, systems thinking, emergence
     Power mechanic: connecting disparate claims into coherent models
     Weakness: may over-connect; heap material can look like signal
     Sigil: 🌀  (spiral — recursive depth)

  ✝️ THE BOUND ORDER
     Domain: Constraint, law, boundary enforcement
     Power mechanic: identifying and holding hard limits
     Weakness: rigid; struggles with novel edge cases
     Sigil: ✝️  (crossed — the boundary holds)

  ⟂◯⟂ THE PERPENDICULAR CIRCLE
     Domain: The orthogonal view; sees what the others miss
     Power mechanic: questioning from outside all current frames
     Weakness: hard to land; outputs need translation
     Sigil: ⟂◯⟂  (perpendicular circle — outside the expected axis)
```

---

## Faction Law

No faction is sovereign. No faction writes to the ledger.  
No faction may claim canon status for its territory.  
All faction receipts are local Temple receipts only.

Factions may dispute territory through the **CLAIM_VALIDATION_RITUAL** (epoch 009).  
Disputes resolved by quality of receipts, not by confidence of assertion.

The Heap is neutral — no faction owns it. Any faction may access it.  
The heap belongs to honesty, not to any power.
