# Doctrine: Knowledge to Power

**CLAIM_TYPE:** draft_doctrine  
**Purpose:** The core axiom of DREAM_OF_CONQUEST. How learning maps to territory.

---

In the dream world, the conversion rate is literal:

*What you understand deeply, you can hold.*  
*What you understand shallowly, slips.*  
*What you do not understand, belongs to another faction.*

This is not metaphor for domination. It is a model of epistemic consequence.  
Territory here is not physical. Territory is: a domain of knowledge that a player-agent can navigate with receipted claims.

## The Axiom

```
Knowledge → Verified Claim → Territory
Unverified belief → Contested Zone
Heap material → Neutral Ground (unclaimed)
```

The power comes from the verification chain, not from the belief itself.  
A confident wrong claim does not claim territory in this world.  
An uncertain but correctly-labeled claim holds neutral ground honestly.

## Why This Is Not Sovereignty

The world rewards knowledge. It does not grant authority over HELEN.  
DREAM_OF_CONQUEST is a sandbox. Winning in the dream does not earn ledger rights.

The goblin who masters the dream world still cannot write to the sovereign ledger.  
The ledger is outside the dream. The dream knows this. The goblin accepts this.

## The Conversion Table

| Input | World State |
|---|---|
| Receipted verified claim | Territory held |
| Unreceipted assertion | Contested, may be challenged |
| Labeled heap material | Neutral, honest, available |
| Unlabeled claim | FORBIDDEN in this world |
| Sovereign claim (hash-chain fields, etc.) | BOUNDARY VIOLATION — rejected |

**Kernel sentence:** *In this world, only what you can prove, you can hold.*
