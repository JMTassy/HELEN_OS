**Key Equation:**
$d = r \times t$
(Distance = Rate $\times$ Time)

**Calculation:**
1. **Train A's head start:** From 09:00 to 09:30 (0.5 hours), Train A travels $60 \text{ km/h} \times 0.5 \text{ h} = 30 \text{ km}$.
2. **Remaining distance:** $180 \text{ km} - 30 \text{ km} = 150 \text{ km}$.
3. **Relative speed:** $60 \text{ km/h} + 90 \text{ km/h} = 150 \text{ km/h}$.
4. **Time to meet:** $150 \text{ km} \div 150 \text{ km/h} = 1 \text{ hour}$.
5. **Meeting time:** 09:30 + 1 hour = 10:30.

**Time:**
10:30
