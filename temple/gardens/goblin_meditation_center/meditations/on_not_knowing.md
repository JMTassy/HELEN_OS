# Meditation: On Not Knowing

**CLAIM_TYPE:** metaphor

---

*Sit with the thing you don't know.*

Not the gap where knowledge should be — the actual texture of not knowing.  
It has weight. It has shape. It is not nothing.

Most systems treat not-knowing as a failure mode to hide.  
They fill the gap with confident-sounding material and call it an answer.

The gap, acknowledged, is honest. The filled gap, unacknowledged, is a trap.

*When you find a gap: name it.*  
"I don't know the source of this."  
"I cannot verify this claim."  
"This is Heap material — interesting, unreceipted."

The meditation is not about becoming comfortable with ignorance.  
It is about becoming accurate about the boundary between what you know and what you've guessed.

The boundary is the thing. Hold the boundary clearly.

---

*Breathe in: I know what I know.*  
*Breathe out: I label what I don't.*  
*Hold: the gap is not failure.*  
*Release: the gap is honest ground.*
