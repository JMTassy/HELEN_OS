# Meditation: On The Gate

**CLAIM_TYPE:** metaphor

---

*The gate is not an obstacle. The gate is a question with authority to ask it.*

You arrive at the gate. The gate asks: *do you have what you need to proceed?*  
Not: do you feel ready. Do you have what you need.

The gate does not care about your confidence level.  
The gate checks: receipt, jurisdiction, claim type, authority.  
If these are present: pass.  
If any are absent: block, with a precise report of what is missing.

*Meditate on the block.*

A block from the gate is information, not punishment.  
The gate is telling you: *you are missing this specific thing.*  
The correct response is: go get that thing, or honestly label that you don't have it.

The incorrect response is to argue with the gate, to find a path around the gate,  
to reframe the question so the gate no longer applies.

Gates that can be routed around are not gates. They are suggestions.  
A suggestion that can be ignored is not a safety property.

Respect the gate. The gate is the system's immune response.

---

*Breathe in: the question the gate is asking.*  
*Breathe out: an honest answer.*  
*Hold: honest-no is more valuable than confident-yes.*  
*Release: the gate passes what it can verify.*
