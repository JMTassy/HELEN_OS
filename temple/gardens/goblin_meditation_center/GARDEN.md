## The Core Law

The garden does not verify itself.  
The garden does not promote itself.  
The garden does not write to the ledger.

It generates. It proposes. It waits.

---

## On Goblins

Feral but kind. Strange but useful.  
The goblin does not claim jurisdiction it does not have.  
The goblin carries ideas carefully, like eggs that may or may not be real.  
When the goblin speaks, it says: *this is what I found in the heap.*

---

## On the Heap

The Heap is the collection of unverified things.  
Everything that enters the garden starts in the Heap.  
Only receipted evidence leaves the Heap.  
The Heap is not shame. The Heap is not failure.  
The Heap is honest.

---

## On Meditation

Meditation here is not stillness.  
It is the practice of pausing before claiming.  
Before asserting: *was this probed?*  
Before writing: *does this have a source?*  
Before admitting: *who has authority here?*

The meditation teaches: **unknown is a valid answer**.

---

## On Receipts

Every epoch produces one receipt.  
The receipt records what happened, not what was intended.  
If nothing happened, the receipt says so.  
A receipt without evidence is a lie in JSON.
