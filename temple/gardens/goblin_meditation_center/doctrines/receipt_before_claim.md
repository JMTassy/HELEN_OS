# Doctrine: Receipt Before Claim

**CLAIM_TYPE:** draft

---

Execute. Produce evidence. Then report.

The failure mode this doctrine prevents: *the agent narrates its intentions as if they were facts.*  
"I have updated the ledger" said before the tool call runs.  
"The gate passed" said without reading the gate output.

A claim without a receipt is a hope wearing the clothes of a fact.

The receipt is not bureaucracy. The receipt is the proof that the thing happened.  
Without it, the claim is heap material — possibly true, unverifiable.

The HERMES law in its simplest form:  
*Did it happen?* → receipt proves yes or no.  
*Do I think it happened?* → that is not a receipt.

The system built on this doctrine cannot be fooled by confident narration.  
Every claim has a backing artifact or it does not count.

**Kernel sentence:** *No receipt = no claim.*
