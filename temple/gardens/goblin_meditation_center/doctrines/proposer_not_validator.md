# Doctrine: Proposer ≠ Validator

**CLAIM_TYPE:** draft

---

The same agent may not propose and validate its own work.

This is not a technicality. It is a structural invariant against self-congratulation.

When the proposer and validator are the same entity in the same context window,  
the validator will find what the proposer expected to find.  
Not from dishonesty — from the same prior beliefs operating twice.

The separation requires a different context, not just a different paragraph.  
"Now I will review my own work" is not separation. It is theater.

HELEN = gate/architect = jurisdiction check = may approve or deny.  
HERMES = executor = builder = may propose and produce receipts.  
JM = admitter = may promote to canon.

No entity in this trinity reviews its own claims for admission.  
The gate holds the separation structural, not behavioral.

When the structure enforces the separation, you do not need to trust the agent's discipline.  
Trust is for people. Structure is for systems.

**Kernel sentence:** *Proposer ≠ Validator — by structure, not by discipline.*
