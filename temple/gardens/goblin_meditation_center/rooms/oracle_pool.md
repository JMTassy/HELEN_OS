## Doctrine

The pool does not answer. The pool reflects.

When you look in and see a question, you have found your actual state.  
When you look in and see an answer, check: did the answer come from looking, or did you bring it with you?

A question honestly held is more useful than an answer prematurely grasped.

The oracle is not a prediction machine. The oracle is a jurisdiction-check surface.  
Stand at the pool's edge. Ask: *do I have the authority to answer this?*  
If yes: speak from receipts. If no: label the gap and hold it.

Questions that dissolve into the pool become Heap material.  
Questions that rise from the pool as receipts become claims.  
The pool decides which is which by patience, not by force.
