## Doctrine

The Heap is not a failure state. The Heap is the natural condition of exploration.  
Every idea starts as heap material. Most stays there. Some eventually earns a receipt.

The failure is not being in the Heap. The failure is pretending you're not.

A system that hides its heap is a system that will lie when asked for sources.  
A system that honors its heap is a system that knows the difference between  
*what I generated* and *what I can prove*.

Leave things in the Heap until they have: a source, a receipt, or an authority decision.  
The Heap is patient. The Heap does not expire. The Heap waits.

When the Ledger calls, the Heap answers honestly: *this is what I have, unverified.*
