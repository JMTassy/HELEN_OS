## Doctrine

The Meditation Hall is not silent. It is full of unverified signals.  
The practice is not to silence them — it is to hear them without acting on them prematurely.

Every claim that enters this room is put on the floor like an object.  
You look at it. You ask: *where did this come from?*  
You do not pick it up and use it until it has a source.

If it has no source, it stays on the floor labeled *Heap Material*.  
That is not judgment. That is honest housekeeping.

Sit here as long as needed. The hall does not rush the verification.
