## Doctrine

The Sanctum is the place receipted work goes to wait.

Not every receipt becomes canon. Most rest here indefinitely.  
That is not failure. That is proportion. The ledger does not need everything; it needs the right things.

What lives in the Sanctum: completed proposals, validated artifacts, finished meditations.  
They carry their receipts. They do not demand admission.

The gate between the Sanctum and canon is held by the Admitter, not by HERMES, not by HELEN.  
Nothing in the Sanctum is canon. Nothing in the Sanctum is lost.

To sit in the Sanctum is to be complete without being admitted.  
Complete is enough. Complete is honorable.

This garden is in the Sanctum. It waits with patience, holding its receipts, asking nothing.
