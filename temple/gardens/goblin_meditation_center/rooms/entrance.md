## Doctrine

Before you enter, answer three questions:  
Which surface are you on? What authority do you actually hold? What claim type is this?

The entrance does not check your credentials. It reminds you that you have some.  
If you cannot answer these questions, you may still enter — but you must label your ignorance.

*Unknown* is a valid answer here. *Guessed* is not the same as *known*.

The door opens inward. The receipts stay on your person.
