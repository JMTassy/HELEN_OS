## What This Is

AVALON is a subsandbox of `goblin_garden_conquest`. It is bounded by the same horizon as its parent: NON_SOVEREIGN, NO_SHIP, UNDERWARREN_SAFE.

The ledger does not move here. AVALON generates no sovereign receipts, touches no kernel paths, and claims no authority over HELEN or its constitutional layer.

AVALON is a **dream island** — a 30-epoch WULmoji march through four faction domains converging on the Dream Core.
