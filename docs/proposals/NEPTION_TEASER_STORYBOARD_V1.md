# NEPTION TEASER STORYBOARD V1 — "DESCENTE"

## Director's Note

*DESCENTE* dramatizes NEPTION's brand thesis: "l'eau cesse d'être une frontière pour devenir un territoire." The teaser is a vertical descent into the abyss—where a biomimetic maritime platform exists as a living organism—then an ascent toward light and the Mediterranean horizon. Every frame earns the reveal of the wordmark. Visual language: abyssal navy (#050c14) as the ground; letterhead gold (#c8a55b) appears *only* as earned light (sun refracting through water, the horizon glow, the seal underline). Foam-white (#eef4f7) for typography in Didot serif. The camera moves are slow, weighted, patient—influence over action. No faces, no logos until the seal. No neon, no corporate teal, no stock drone clichés.

## PROMPT CARDS

### BEAT 1: SURFACE

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
A sunlit sea surface photographed from 30cm below the waterline at solar noon. Gentle, 60cm swells refract golden light into radiant caustic patterns on the water's underside. The frame is filled with translucent turquoise (Pantone Aqua, fading to cream white where light is strongest). Camera positioned horizontally. Volumetric god-rays stream downward from each swell crest. Film grain subtle; color grading: crushed blacks, lifted shadows, warm highlights (#c8a55b cast on the light geometry). Lens: 35mm equivalent, f/4 aperture. Cinematic, patient, meditative.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Static camera, locked on the refraction geometry. Gentle swell motion: the light-pattern undulates across the frame for 2.5s, breathing with the ocean's rhythm. No camera movement. Allow the caustic patterns to hypnotize. Depth of field: slight softness on the foreground caustics to emphasize the haze/particulate.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No sun disk visible. No people. No debris. No bubbles (avoid the cliché of "underwater" particle spray). No commercial logos or text. No bright neon or sickly teal. No fast cuts. No drone motion.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
Frame the brightest caustic cluster in the center-left; allow the upper swell to dominate the top third. Vertical reframing preserves the "looking up" sense crucial to the descent-ascent metaphor.

### BEAT 2: DESCENT

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
Volumetric god-rays pierce downward through a darkening water column. Suspended particulate (organic detritus, plankton, minerals) catches and scatters the narrowing shafts of light. Water color: turquoise at the top, deepening to cobalt, then navy blue (#050c14 approaching at the frame's edge). Lens flare is strictly forbidden; instead, the light shafts are rendered as solid, sculptural rays. Film stock feel: slightly desaturated, contrast stretched. Lens: 50mm equivalent, slight vignette. Lighting: volumetric depth (god-rays cast shadows on particles). A sense of serene descent, not fear.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Camera sinks vertically downward at 0.3m/s (very slow). The god-rays shift as the camera descends—shafts move across the frame as the light geometry changes. Particulate swirls gently in 3D space, responding to the camera's passage. Water darkens progressively. No sudden cuts. A single, hypnotic descent lasting 3s. The camera's vertical axis is the metaphorical spine of the entire piece.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No bioluminescence yet. No creatures. No machinery. No bubbles or air pockets (this is the deep, silent descent). No color grading shortcuts (neon blues, oversaturated cyans). No lens flares. No fast-motion particle effects.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
Letterbox the god-rays into a narrower vertical column. The vertical framing amplifies the descent sensation. Edges darken (vignette tightens).

### BEAT 3: REVEAL

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
A biomimetic floating platform revealed from below, silhouetted against the narrowing shaft of surface light. Hull form inspired by coral and manta-ray morphology—organic curves, modular pods, no hard industrial edges or foundations. The silhouette is the hero: no interior details yet, pure form against luminance. Color: mostly dark (platform is shadowed), but the outline catches a thin line of rim-light from the surface glow. Lens: 24mm equivalent, slightly wide, to make the platform feel monumental. Depth: platform is perhaps 50m below surface (estimated by light attenuation). A sense of discovery, revelation, awe.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Camera continues sinking, then performs a slow upward pan—from the platform's base/foundation (or what looks like its base) up through its silhouette to its crest, which meets the brightest descending light. The pan takes 3.5s and is purposeful, unhurried. The platform *grows* on screen as the light reveals more of its form. At the pan's peak, the platform is backlit, forming a heroic silhouette. This is the climactic reveal of the film's central object.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No visible anchor cables or industrial infrastructure. No robotic or mechanical details. No human figures or diving equipment. No neon glow-ups. No corporate logos on the platform. No generic ocean-base clichés (no domed structures, no caisson-style legs). The form must read as *alive*, not engineered.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
Frame the platform's apex at the vertical center. Allow the descending light shaft to form a halo above it. The full platform silhouette occupies roughly 60% of the frame vertically.

### BEAT 4: LIVING

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
The biomimetic platform now framed as a living, breathing organism. Soft bioluminescent accents (pale gold #c8a55b, very muted, never neon) pulse gently along ridges and pod-joints—suggesting neural pathways or biological luminescence. A school of fish (2–4 individual fish, not a coordinated swarm) moves through or past the structure. Water moves visibly *through* the platform's modular geometry—no solid walls, but permeable surfaces. The platform sways slightly, responding to deep-ocean currents. Color: mostly abyssal navy (#050c14 dominant), with the gold accents as earned light points. Lens: 35mm equivalent, gentle orbital float.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Camera performs a slow, gentle orbit around the platform (perhaps 180° arc over 3s). The orbit is not a dramatic circle, but a subtle float—drifting slightly up, around, and down. As the camera moves, the bioluminescent accents pulse (soft breathing rhythm, ~0.8s cycle). Water distortion and particle motion continue. Fish pass through the frame, unhurried. A single cello note sustains and gently rises (see Audio section). The overall impression: the platform is resting, alive, at peace in the deep.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No mechanical animation or industrial sounds. No neon bioluminescence (no cyan, no acid-green). No aggressive fish or predator imagery. No spinning or fast rotations. No lens flares or bloom. No visible support structures or cables.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
Keep the platform centered but slightly offset to the left to allow space for the fish school to "enter" from the right. The bioluminescent accents should peak in brightness at the frame center.

### BEAT 5: ASCENT

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
Camera rises from the abyss toward the sea surface, which now glows with the golden hour (sunset). Water lightens from navy (#050c14) through cobalt to turquoise, then to a pale golden-white at the surface. The platform is now seen from above/horizontally as it recedes downward, appearing serene and small against the vastness of the sea. In the distance (very faint, soft-focused): the Mediterranean coastline and Monaco/Riviera silhouette at dusk. The horizon line is warm and soft, filled with diffuse golden light. Lens: 50mm equivalent, slight lift in shadows. Film feel: nostalgic, peaceful.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Camera rises vertically upward for 3.5s at a slow, constant rate (0.3m/s, matching the descent speed for poetic symmetry). Water lightens progressively. The platform shrinks on screen as the camera moves away and up. At the midpoint (1.75s), the horizon enters the frame and grows in prominence. By frame-end, the platform is small in the lower portion of the frame, the horizon dominates the upper half, and we are breaking through to the surface. No splash or dramatic water-line crossing—a smooth, seamless emergence.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No sun disk. No people or boats. No commercial structures. No jarring color shifts. No fast camera motion. No celebratory or triumphant music (stay meditative). No lens flares.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
For social crop: position the platform in the lower-center third, the horizon and sky occupying the upper two-thirds. The vertical framing reads as "transcendence" or "emergence."

### BEAT 6: MANIFESTO

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
Deep navy/near-black background (#050c14 or true black with subtle blue cast). Centered, in foam-white Didot serif (high-contrast, elegant, spaced):  
*"L'eau n'est plus une frontière.  
Elle devient territoire."*  

A single hairline rule in letterhead gold (#c8a55b) sits beneath the text, perfectly centered. The layout is austere, peaceful, Talmudic (margins of silence around the text). No decorative elements. Film grain is minimal. Aspect: 16:9, text occupies roughly the center 40% of the frame vertically.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Static frame. The text appears (fade-in or reveal, 0.3s), holds for 2.2s. The gold hairline rule draws itself from left to right (line animation, 0.7s, starting at 0.5s into the shot). After the line completes, hold all elements for 0.5s, then fade to black over 0.3s. This is the conceptual heart: the claim is *stated*, not dramatized.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No moving backgrounds. No video texture behind text. No drop shadows on type. No sound effects (silence or sustained drone only). No commentary voiceover. No secondary text or logos.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
For social: keep the text centered vertically. The line animation becomes a critical accent (a "check mark" or "seal" of the claim).

### BEAT 7: SEAL

**IMAGE PROMPT**  
Deep abyss (black or near-black, #050c14). Centered, in foam-white spaced serif caps:  
*NEPTION®*  

Below, a smaller all-caps monospace tagline in foam-white:  
*PROMOTEUR D'INFLUENCE MARITIME · MONACO*  

A single, elegant gold underline (#c8a55b) begins at the left edge of "NEPTION" and draws itself across the full width of the wordmark over 0.7s. Once the underline completes, it holds steady. The wordmark is the earned reward of the descent and ascent. Film grain: none. Spacing is generous and refined. Aspect: 16:9.

**VIDEO PROMPT**  
Static frame. Wordmark and tagline fade in (0.2s), hold. After 0.8s, the gold underline draws itself from left to right (0.7s animation, easing slow). After the line completes, hold all elements for 0.5s. Then fade to pure black/abyss over 0.3s. Total: 2.0s. The line-draw is the climactic moment: the brand is *sealed*.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT**  
No spinning logos. No lens flares. No gradient backgrounds. No secondary colors. No additional imagery or icons. No animated swooshes or sound effects (keep audio minimal). No transparency or blur on the wordmark. No commercial disclaimers.

**9:16 CROP NOTE**  
Wordmark centered; allow equal margin above and below. The underline becomes a horizontal "anchor" in the vertical composition.

## AUDIO

**Suggested Sound Bed (Royalty-Free / Original)**

- **0.0–2.5s (SURFACE):** Ambient ocean ambience with sub-bass hum (20–60 Hz, very low, felt rather than heard). Gentle wave sound (actual ocean recording, minimal compression). No music.
  
- **2.5–5.5s (DESCENT):** Fade out ocean ambience. Enter a single sustained cello note (approximately B2 or C#3, a mournful, meditative pitch). The note enters at ~0.5s into the descent and sustains softly. Layered beneath: a very faint theremin-like pad (no actual theremin; use a synth with slow LFO), creating an ethereal sense of depth. Sub-bass hum continues.

- **5.5–8.5s (REVEAL):** Cello note holds and very subtly rises in pitch (portamento, over 1s) as the platform silhouette is revealed. A second cello line enters (a fourth or fifth above the first) in pizzicato, soft, 2–3 plucks as accents on the reveal. Theremin pad continues.

- **8.5–11.5s (LIVING):** Cello sustains a held note (peak emotion). Gentle pulsing pad (soft pad swells and fades in rhythm, ~0.8s cycle, matching the bioluminescent pulse). Ambient water sounds return, very subtle. A single bell-tone (Tibetan singing bowl, struck once, reverb tail extending) marks the moment of maximum serenity.

- **11.5–15.0s (ASCENT):** Cello note very gently descends in pitch (portamento downward) as the camera rises. The theremin pad fades. Ambient ocean hum returns, gradually increasing in presence. At ~14s (near surface), a breathy whoosh (recorded sound of the air/water interface, not exaggerated). All music fades.

- **15.0–17.5s (MANIFESTO):** Silence (or near-silence with only the barest ambient hum, fade to 0). The text is stated in silence. This is the power: the claim does not need accompaniment.

- **17.5–19.5s (SEAL):** A single, very low sub-bass drone tone (approximately 30 Hz, felt as vibration) enters at the moment the wordmark appears. It holds steady as the gold underline draws itself. At the line's completion, a brief echo of the cello's highest note (very short, 0.2s) marks the seal. The sub-bass continues under and fades to silence as the frame fades to black.

**Technical Notes:**
- All music should be original composition or royalty-free (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or original score).
- No licensed music or recognizable classical pieces.
- No vocal elements.
- No percussion impacts or dramatic stabs (stay meditative and patient).
- Master mix: -6dB headroom; LUF target: -14 (broadcast standard).

## TRANSITIONS

**Match-Cut Logic (Descent→Ascent Axis)**

All beats are connected by the vertical light axis:
- **SURFACE → DESCENT:** The brightest caustic becomes the descending god-rays. Light geometry is *continuous*.
- **DESCENT → REVEAL:** The narrowing god-rays converge on the platform's silhouette. The platform is *revealed by the light*, not introduced separately.
- **REVEAL → LIVING:** No cut; the camera's upward pan transforms into the orbit. The platform remains the focal anchor.
- **LIVING → ASCENT:** The camera's float transitions seamlessly into the upward rise. The platform shrinks naturally as distance increases (perspective, not a cut).
- **ASCENT → MANIFESTO:** At the surface, a fade to black (0.3s), then the text field fades in over black. This is a threshold: we leave the sensory world and enter the conceptual.
- **MANIFESTO → SEAL:** A fade through black (0.2s). Both text frames occupy the same compositional space (centered), reinforcing the connection of claim to brand.
- **SEAL → BLACK:** Final fade to pure black/abyss. Full circle: we end where we descended, but transformed by the knowledge of what exists below.

**Spatial Coherence:**
- The descent, reveal, orbit, and ascent form a continuous spatial journey (virtual single take, or seamless cuts respecting 180° rule and light direction).
- The two text frames (MANIFESTO, SEAL) are outside the spatial realm—they are revealed *after* the journey.
- No jarring cuts. Transitions are motivated by camera movement, light direction, or thematic threshold (sensory → conceptual).
