## G — the corpus is almost a natural G_R (relation graph)

The TOC (Hermeticum → Cabala → Picatrix → Templars → Rosicrucians → Freemasonry → Mesmer → Tarot/Egypt → Druid revival → Spiritualism → Theosophy → Golden Dawn → Crowley → Wicca) is exactly the terrain where **symbolic proximity manufactures false lineage**. Core law:

    ChronologicalAdjacency ⊬ GenealogicalContinuity

Five distinct edge-mechanisms the corpus forces apart (HELEN must keep them typed, never coerce upward):

    TRANSMISSION        — a document genuinely passes A → B
    REDISCOVERY         — old material found after a discontinuity
    REINTERPRETATION    — old material given new meaning
    SYNTHESIS           — several traditions recombined
    RETROACTIVE_GENEALOGY — a recent tradition narrates itself as ancient

Without these types: `Rediscovery + Similarity + ClaimedOrigin` silently becomes `Ancestry`.

## H — the chiddush (the deep one: a THIRD graph)

Beyond G_R (relation) and G_W (warrant), the occult corpus forces a **belief graph**:

    G_H = graph of historical transmission        (what actually passed)
    G_B = graph of BELIEFS about transmission     (what traditions claim passed)

    Path(G_B) ⊬ Path(G_H)          — a claimed lineage is not a real one
    BUT Path(G_B) has real historical EFFECTS — the belief itself propagates and shapes practice.

> **Myth may be historically false as ancestry, yet historically real as influence.**

    FalseAncestryClaim ∧ RealInfluenceOfClaim   is NOT a contradiction.

Worked instance: an occultist invents an Egyptian origin for Tarot in **1781**. The Egyptian ancestry is historically **false** (`Tarot --historical--> Renaissance Europe ~1418`); the *1781 attribution* is itself a **real historical event** that influenced centuries of occultists. So:

    Tarot --historical--> RenaissanceEurope       (G_H, real)
    Tarot --later_attribution--> Egypt            (G_B, real AS A BELIEF-EVENT)
    Egypt --ancestry--> Tarot                     (FORBIDDEN — never built from a late claim)

    RetroactiveAttribution ⊬ HistoricalAncestry
