where it always held:


  invisible to the sovereign ledger — which was never meant to record it.
- The χ_god temptation (the world willing its own conquest) is a **forbidden
  self-sealing claim**, not a forbidden thought. The dream may host it; the

  curvature, and holonomy remain unproven and unassumed (Transport Vol II Ch.5–6).
  This is an **analogy recorded**, a dictionary — not a construction.
