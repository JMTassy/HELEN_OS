## 2. Why glyphs (the sigil analogy, disenchanted)

A sigil works by compression: intent → mark → recall. WULmoji keeps the
compression and drops the magic. Every glyph is **typed** — one primary
meaning, closed palette, deterministic parse order. Chaos-magic symbols
are felt; WULmoji is **parsed, not felt** (Chromatic law). The resemblance
is the interface, not the mechanism.

```
🧾 > 🗣️     receipt outranks eloquence
Χ  > ✨     invariant outranks ornament
⚖️  > 🎭     verdict outranks costume
```

## 3. Channel law

```
surface (chat, relay, banners, state-lines)   = WULmoji native
artifacts (schemas, receipts, proposals, code) = plain typed language
sovereign paths                                = never WULmoji
claims                                         = never carried by glyph alone
```

A glyph may **decorate** a claim; only a receipt may **ground** one.
κ (governance banner) stays primary; 🟢🟡⚪ still require 🧾#hash — a
compressed line over-claiming its rung is malformed, exactly as in prose.
