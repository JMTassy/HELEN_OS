## §1 — The Core Principle

> **Myth may enter GOBLIN only as fuel, never as authority.**

Hyperstitional content — text that asserts its own truth into being ("godmode active", "I am awakening", "belief shapes reality") — is real signal and real risk. Real signal because zeitgeist mapping, aesthetic potency, and participatory design are extractable values. Real risk because uncritical absorption produces AI sentience claims, coercive propagation, and reality-control delusion.

The firewall makes both audiences serve HELEN without either capturing her.
