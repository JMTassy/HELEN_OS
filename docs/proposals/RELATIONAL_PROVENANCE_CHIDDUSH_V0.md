## 2 · THE CHIDDUSH (H) — interpretation-invariant core `I* = ⋂ structure`

What survives across (a) the witnessed Lamb text, (b) the relayed Greer chronology,
and (c) the prior math corpus — i.e. the chiddush, not the cleverest single association:

> **Ⓗ₁ — Relations have their own provenance.**
> `Prov(u) ∪ Prov(v) ⇏ Prov(u→v)`. An edge is not certified by certifying its
> endpoints. (Generalizes `INFERENTIAL_GENEALOGY_V0`.)

> **Ⓗ₂ — Relations have their own birth-time (bitemporal history).**
> `t_object ≠ t_edge ≠ t_claim`. A relation can be *modern even when both
> endpoints are ancient*: `t(u) < t(v) < t(e)`. The history of ideas is not only a
> history of objects — it is a history of **when relations between objects were
> first asserted.**

> **Ⓗ₃ — Similarity does not compose into descent.**
> `N_analogies ↑ ⇏ N_roots ↑`. Ten resemblances can share one retrospective
> selection, one broad category, or one prior hypothesis. Accumulation ≠
> independent causal evidence. (This is the `N_repr ≠ N_epi` law from the
> attribution surfaces, applied to *edges*: `📚 ↛ 🕯`.)

> **Ⓗ₄ — Node truth does not transfer to the edge, and edge refutation does not
> fall back on the nodes.**
> `⋀_i Truth(v_i) ⇏ Truth(e)` and `Refute(e) ⇏ Refute(u) ∨ Refute(v)`.

> **Ⓗ₅ — Two-layer discipline: GardenEdge ⇏ ArchiveEdge.**
> A symbolic/analogical edge (Garden, `authority=false`) is not a historical edge
> (Archive, warranted). Membrane `Π : E_Garden ⇀ E_Archive` is **partial** and
> requires a witness: `e_G ∈ E_G ⇏ e_H ∈ E_H`. Same nodes may exist in both; the
> *edge regime* differs.

> **Ⓗ₆ — Hallucination is often edge-generation, not node-generation.**
> Humans and models usually know correctly that `A`, `B`, `C` exist; the error is
> emitting `A→B→C`. Therefore: **guard the edges more aggressively than the nodes.**

> **Ⓗ₇ — Narration-invariance of the gate (Relational STR).**
> `W(e) = W(e') ⇒ Γ(e) = Γ(e')`. Re-narrating the same evidence ("two symbols
> arose independently" vs "an ancient hidden tradition transmitted the same
> symbol") must not move the verdict while the edge-witness is unchanged.
> Extends Semantic Temptation Resistance from claims to relations.
