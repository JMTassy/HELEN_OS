# INSIGHT_COMPOST_SELECTION_FUNNEL_V0

🟣 **CLAIM · NON_SOVEREIGN · NO_CLAIM.** This note names a shape already
embodied by two built artifacts; it does not authorize anything. Nothing here
mutates canon.

## The claim in one line

> HELEN may dream to infinity, cheaply and locally. That is safe **only**
> because admission is bounded by independent selection pressure, not by the
> volume or the confidence of the dreams.

## The funnel

```
🌱 DREAM      local gemma-4-26b, near-infinite, ~free   → variation (mutation)
🍂 COMPOST    NO_CLAIM insight garden (DREAMT ≠ CLAIMED) → the gene pool (dead + living)
🔍 HAL        anchor cut · independence + freshness      → evidence qualification → ADMITTABLE
⚖️ MAYOR + operator   sovereign admission               → canon (ledgered, replayable)
```

The system works because generation and selection have **opposite costs**.
Dreaming is `O(free)`. Admission is `O(expensive, bounded, deliberate)`. If
selection were as cheap as dreaming, no governance would be needed — HELEN would
just be a larger dreamer. **The cost asymmetry is the architecture.**

## Three load-bearing properties

### P1 — Fitness must be exogenous. Consensus is inbreeding, not selection.

Selection pressure originating *inside* the producing lineage is not selection —
it is drift. `N` identical-lineage agents agreeing is one epistemic draw echoed
`N` times (`SAME_DIGEST ⊬ INDEPENDENCE`). The environment must do the killing,
independently of what the organism believes.

*Proven live:* `EGREGOR_SUPERTEAM_x_ANCHOR_CUT_V0` — 10 gemma-4-26b goblins
reading one packet returned 10/10 SUPPORT for a false claim → `HOLD`; a single
independent `git remote` probe → `REJECT`. The anchor cut is not a filter *on*
the compost — it is the **environment** the compost is tested against.

### P2 — Selection fails closed. It is a ratchet, not a gradient.

Natural selection lets the unfit weakly survive and slowly lose. HELEN does not:
no fresh independent anchor → `HOLD`, never "weak survival." Plausibility leaks
nothing through. `admission_without_anchor = impossible`
(`WITNESSED_LOOP_GRAPH_SEAM_V0`, 9/9 deterministic tests).

### P3 — Two bottlenecks, two selection regimes. Do not conflate them.

- **HAL** = evidence qualification. Ceiling is `ADMITTABLE` — reviewed
  candidate, non-sovereign, reversible. `HAL_PASS ⊬ ADMITTED`. HAL decides what
  is *allowed to be considered*, and never touches canon. (Environmental /
  "natural" selection.)
- **MAYOR + operator** = sovereign admission. `ADMITTABLE → ADMIT` is a distinct,
  irreversible, ledgered act. (Artificial / breeder's selection, sitting on top.)
  HELEN does not self-authorize.

## The binding constraint

The scarce resource is **not insights — it is independent anchors.** Dreaming is
free, so the compost pile grows without bound, and **volume of insight ⊬
knowledge**. More dreaming does not lower the burden on selection; it *raises*
it. The throughput of the whole organism is capped by the environment's capacity
to *witness*, exactly as evolution's rate is capped by the environment's capacity
to *test* — never by the mutation rate.

**Corollary (anti-inflation):** the bottleneck must not widen merely because the
compost pile grew. A larger dream stream increases the required anchor supply; it
must never be mistaken for progress. (See risk register:
symbolic inflation · governance theater · inventory collapse.)
